# Known risks and trust boundaries

Issue: DOM-009 / #233. Reviewed source baseline: `4b2ee5e89763450078767d564a07f73fabfe2d63`
(main, 2026-10-14); implementation statements re-checked against `03ac74a`. Status: **proposed risk register; second-person review pending**.
The likelihood and impact ratings below are qualitative judgments for the synthetic
testnet demo, not measured probabilities. None records an organizational risk
acceptance or an external security audit.

## Scope and authority

Funds reside in Safe wallets. Calls made through `TreasuryPilotModule` enforce
its current approval, mandate, budgets, floors, payee and replay rules. A Safe
owner transaction or another enabled module is a separate authority path. An
application role, an AI answer, or a backend policy decision alone cannot create
a valid on-chain approval. Governance exceptions are Safe T transactions, not
AI grants or a second typed exception signature.

The [system architecture](../SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md) describes the target system.
At this baseline, contracts, deterministic engine primitives, org-scoped RBAC,
AI input controls, deployment/receipt helpers, and the EXE-005 signer boundary have
implementations. Sepolia contracts are not deployed yet (`deployments/11155111.json` is
`configured_only`). Worker business execution, recovery and several invoice, policy and
reconciliation integrations remain incomplete. Controls described as planned below must be
demonstrated before enabling autonomous execution.

## Release review

1. A second team member reviews ratings, mitigation claims and proposed owners.
2. Record reviewed risk decisions in the PR; do not infer acceptance from this file.
3. Link raw checks to their exact source revision, chain/block and execution mode.
4. Reassess changed contract, signer, policy, AI tool, deployment and data boundaries.
5. Report any newly discovered exploitable vulnerability privately according to
   [CONTRIBUTING](../../CONTRIBUTING.md); this register documents architectural
   residual risks and integration/release requirements.
